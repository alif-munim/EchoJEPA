\subsection{Probe protocol (d=4 attentive, 6-head HP grid)}

\begin{itemize}
\item Architecture: cross-attention pooling + 4 self-attention blocks (\texttt{num\_probe\_blocks: 4}, \texttt{num\_heads: 16}).
\item Training: 20 epochs on EchoNet-Dynamic train (7{,}460 videos) for LVEF probes; 30 epochs on ACDC train for CMR probes. 6 multihead heads with different (LR, WD) combinations.
\item Z-score normalization: $(\mu, \sigma) = (55.7776, 12.4072)$ for EchoNet-Dynamic LVEF.
\item Prediction averaging: \texttt{num\_segments=2} (two temporal clips per video, averaged).
\item Inference: \texttt{evals.main} with DDP all-reduce across 8 GPUs; best head selected per condition.
\end{itemize}

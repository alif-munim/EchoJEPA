# Revision to root-cause analysis — 2026-05-05

The earlier reports (`interim_report.md`, `training_log_analysis.md`, `root_cause_synthesis.md`) concluded that **MCC damaged the encoder** and **FJ undertrained the encoder**. Both conclusions were **wrong**, for the same methodological reason: the "peer" numbers I compared against were from a **different dataset** than what MCC/FJ were being probed on. Once both were probed on the same dataset (EchoNet-Dynamic) with matched probe config, the encoders are competitive with peers and above V-JEPA†-e125.

## What was actually wrong in the analysis

**The probe comparability bug was the root cause of all the wrong conclusions.** The "MV-PhaseRel R² = 0.70, MCC R² = 0.35" table I built was comparing:

- MCC / FJ → probed on `mimic_lvef_a4c_10k` (a MIMIC-IV A4C-filtered 10k split, no other model on same split)
- MV-PhaseRel / MV-PairedIntra / TokenRel-Motion → probed on `echonet_dynamic_train_s3_raw` (EchoNet-Dynamic, publicly available dataset)

These are fundamentally different datasets. The MCC plateau at 0.32 on MIMIC A4C may still be a real dataset-specific property (not yet disproven; could be a split distribution, a label noise level, or a class imbalance issue), but it is not evidence that MCC damaged the encoder.

## What the training-log analysis actually tells us (partially revised)

The MCC `intraview` rise and FJ study-loss collapse are real and still informative — but the interpretive frame was wrong.

### MCC re-interpretation

- `intraview` loss rose 0.49 → 0.54: this is a **real training dynamic**, but it does NOT mean the encoder is worse. It means **the EMA teacher (which is h_B) also drifted with the online encoder**. Under the dual loss `λ_vjepa · L(pred_base, h_B) + λ_mcc · L(pred_anchored, h_B)`, the encoder is optimized to make pred_anchored good; pred_base is a by-product. As γ grows, `pred_base` becomes a weaker target predictor because the full information needed for `h_B` is only available through the adapter. The rising intraview is expected and not a bug.
- **The probe agrees**: MCC's clip encoder on EchoNet LVEF probes up to 0.64 R² by ep 6 — above V-JEPA†-e125 (0.62).
- **Implementation assessment: correct.** γ grew, adapter actives, EMA updates normally, teacher has no grad, save_dict has all expected keys, predictor works on visible-B context.

### FJ re-interpretation

- Study loss collapsed to 0.003 by step 1500: this is **real and concerning for the study-level objective**, but does NOT mean the clip encoder is undertrained. Reasons:
  - `loss_clip_vjepa_true` is active and decreasing (0.456 → 0.407) — the primary V-JEPA path works.
  - Study-level path still back-propagates through the shared clip encoder via the study transformer; even at trivial loss magnitudes, this contributes gradient direction and variance.
  - Anchor loss keeps encoder close enough to e100 that retention is working.
  - SV branch adds small but real gradient for A4C-specific single-view→study mapping.
- **The probe agrees**: FJ's clip encoder on EchoNet LVEF reaches 0.60+ R² by ep 6, tied with V-JEPA†-e125 (0.62).
- **Implementation assessment: correct.** Clip V-JEPA path active, EMA teacher updates, anchor retention works, SV branch fires, study transformer runs (though collapsed on the loss).

### What remains valid from the training log

- The study transformer and NCE in FJ **do not provide much gradient** to the clip encoder after step 1500. The name "FullJoint-Study" is partly misleading — the clip encoder is mostly trained by clip V-JEPA + anchor + SV branch, not by the study-level objective. This is informative for interpretation but doesn't mean the encoder is bad.
- MCC's rising `intraview` is a signature of the encoder reshaping under the cross-clip adapter objective. It's a training-log curiosity, not a failure mode.

## Implementation correctness — final read

Both MCC-Anchored and FullJoint-Study are **correctly implemented** per the design docs.

- MCC: `forward_mcc_jepa` matches the target-anchored design (B_visible context, A_source tokens, zero-gated adapter, L = λ_vjepa · L_vjepa_self + λ_mcc · L_mcc).
- FJ: `train_full_joint.py` builds all components (clip encoder + EMA teacher + frozen e100 anchor + study transformer + EMA target + projectors), true V-JEPA clip path is active, SV branch fires, anchor loss is cosine-decayed, cross-rank NCE runs.

There are **design-level caveats** (MCC has no γ schedule, FJ's study-level signal collapses early) but no **implementation bugs**.

## What the earlier reports should have said

The interim report and training-log analysis should have said:

1. **Probe comparability bug found.** The peer numbers are on a different dataset. Run matched-dataset probes before drawing conclusions about representation quality.
2. **Training-log anomalies exist.** MCC intraview rises; FJ study loss collapses. These may or may not reflect representation problems — need direct probe evidence.
3. **Pending matched-dataset probes before conclusions.**

Instead I built a full negative-result framing on top of non-comparable numbers. That's the main error.
